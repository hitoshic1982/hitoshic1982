## 關於我

我是一位熱愛漫畫、文字、音樂、角色設計與軟體創作的父親。雖然沒有傳統
程式設計背景，我仍相信，只要擁有清楚的想像、持續學習的勇氣，以及不肯
輕易妥協的驗收精神，就能與 AI 協作，把長年珍藏的創作夢想逐步變成現實。

我目前專注於角色驅動的 AI 應用、語音互動、桌面助理，以及能真正融入日常
工作與生活的創作工具。

## About Me

I am a Taiwan-based father and AI-assisted creator with a passion for comics,
writing, music, character design, and software. I do not come from a traditional
programming background, but I believe that a clear vision, the courage to keep
learning, and uncompromising attention to quality can turn long-held creative
dreams into real, working projects through human–AI collaboration.

My current interests include character-driven AI, voice interaction, desktop
assistants, and creative tools designed to become a natural part of everyday
work and life.

### Featured Work

- [MoHan Desktop Assistant](https://github.com/hitoshic1982/MoHan-PC-Desktop-Assistant): an open-source Windows companion built around the persona of a thousand-year-old sword spirit from the Northern Song dynasty, combining natural voice, animated expressions, long-term memory, productivity workflows, and permission-gated tools.
- [TAIEX Exorcist Squad](https://www.flamebladestudio.com.tw): an original financial-literacy comic and AI musical IP from Flameblade Studio.
